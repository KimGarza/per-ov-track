# Faith artwork

Project assets created with the built-in image generation tool. PNGs are optimized app-sized copies; the originals remain outside the repository. Both have alpha transparency.

- `assets/faith/moon-moth.png`: 384 × 256. Decorative artwork enabled by the palette's celestial decoration flag.
- The verse control uses a built-in Bible book symbol. No hands artwork is included in the app.

Final moon/moth prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: decorative corner illustration for a gothic Christian mobile app theme, not a texture background. Create an elegant ivory white full moon with a subtle luminous concentric halo, a scattering of delicate four-point stars, and one beautiful moth with open wings near the lower right of the moon. Quiet devotional, celestial gothic, antique silver and ivory line engraving with restrained soft glow. Wide horizontal composition, generous empty transparent space, no lettering or symbols other than moon, halo, stars and moth. Isolated artwork on a truly transparent background, no solid sky, no surrounding scenery, no gradient backdrop. Legible as a decorative 160px corner illustration on a dark plum black app background.

No background texture was added. A supplied texture can later be packaged as a local asset and registered in the theme rather than hardcoded into individual screens.
